My story

Why a future game developer chose a business minor

The problem. I want to make games, and games are never just code. Someone has to plan the team, the budget and the audience, and I did not know how a business is run or what qualities running one takes.

The solution. I added a BBA minor to find out. I am learning to write, speak and present, and to see how organisations work, so the games I build one day can reach players and survive as a business.
