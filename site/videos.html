Recommended videos & lectures

Talks that changed how I think about communication. Each has a one-line takeaway in my own words.

▶ Watch on TED

Amy Cuddy, Your Body Language May Shape Who You Are (TEDGlobal 2012)

Why I recommend itStanding tall before a big moment can help me feel more confident, not just look it.

Joe Navarro, The Power of Nonverbal Communication (TEDxManchester)

Why I recommend itRead clusters of cues, not one gesture, and notice how comfort or discomfort shows in the body.

▶ Watch on TED

Victoria Turk, How to Write an Email (No, Really) (TEDxAthens)

Why I recommend itA good email makes its purpose and the reader's next step obvious.

Hans Rosling, The Best Stats You've Ever Seen (TED2006)

Why I recommend itMy personal brand is how clearly I show who I am, and how consistently, everywhere people find me.
